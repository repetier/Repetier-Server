# Privacy Policy Repetier-Server

Last updated: 12 September 2026 — valid from version 2.0.0

## 1. The most important points first

Repetier-Server runs on your own hardware. The software contains no telemetry, no analytics or
tracking functions and no advertising.

Data about your printers, print jobs, files or your use of Repetier-Server is not transmitted to
us, nor is it analysed, sold or used for advertising purposes by us.

Until 30 June 2027, Repetier-Server automatically connects to a server operated by the provider
solely in order to check whether program updates are available. Installation files and updates
are provided via GitHub.

From 30 June 2027, Repetier-Server will automatically stop checking for updates and stop sending
push messages to Repetier-Informer. After that date, Repetier-Server no longer transmits any
personal data to the provider's servers on its own initiative.

## 3. Data stored locally

All data that you create in Repetier-Server or that arises during operation – in particular user
accounts and passwords, print jobs, G-code and model files, print history, time-lapse recordings,
camera images and log files – is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

For this locally processed data, the respective operator of the Repetier-Server installation is
responsible, insofar as data protection law applies to that use. Where private individuals use
the software exclusively for personal or household purposes, the GDPR generally does not apply.

Repetier-Server works as a web server and, depending on your network configuration, may also be
reachable from the internet. We recommend enabling user authentication and allowing external
access only over a secured connection.

## 4. Update check and downloads

Until 30 June 2027, Repetier-Server retrieves a small file from a server operated by the provider
at regular intervals in order to check whether a newer program version is available. It is
technically apparent which platform the file is requested for, for example Linux Intel, Linux ARM
or Windows.

As with any internet connection, the IP address of your internet connection is technically
transmitted to the server. We do not store it in access logs and do not evaluate it for analytics
purposes. Installation or device identifiers, licence data, printer data or usage information are
not transmitted.

The processing serves to provide the update check and to operate the service securely. The legal
basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The update server is operated at Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen,
Germany, as a processor pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

Transmitting the IP address is neither required by law nor by contract, but it is technically
necessary for any internet request. The update check cannot be disabled inside Repetier-Server.
If access to the update server is blocked at network or firewall level, no automatic update check
is possible.

Installation files and updates are provided via GitHub Releases and GitHub Assets. When a download
is started, the file is retrieved directly from GitHub. In doing so, GitHub processes in particular
the technically necessary IP address and, where applicable, further technical information in
accordance with its own privacy policy. The provider of Repetier-Server receives no personal
information about who downloaded a particular file.

From 30 June 2027, Repetier-Server version 2.0.0 and later will automatically stop checking for
updates. The update page will then only show a link to the public
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/repetier/Repetier-Server). A connection to GitHub is
established only when the user follows that link or starts a download offered there.

## 5. Web interface

The Repetier-Server user interface is displayed in your browser. A technically necessary session
cookie and local browser storage are used for login and interface settings.

This information stays between your browser and your own Repetier-Server installation and is not
transmitted to us.

Insofar as the storage is technically necessary to provide the functions you have requested,
consent under Section 25(2)(2) of the German Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG) is not
required.

## 6. Connections set up by you

If you explicitly configure it to do so, Repetier-Server can communicate with further systems or
services, for example with Repetier-Host, Repetier-Informer, Pushover, IFTTT, Home Assistant,
ioBroker or an MQTT broker.

These connections are not active by default. If you set up such a connection, the data required
for it is transmitted to the system or service you selected.

For external services such as Pushover or IFTTT, the privacy policy of the respective provider
applies. Home Assistant, ioBroker and MQTT brokers can also be operated locally or on systems you
choose yourself.

Repetier-Informer is a separate service of the same provider. If you enable push messages to
Repetier-Informer, the data required for this is transmitted to that service. For details, please
refer to the privacy policy of Repetier-Informer.

Sending push messages to Repetier-Informer will be discontinued automatically on 30 June 2027.

## 7. Versions adapted by printer manufacturers

If you obtain Repetier-Server or a ready-made system image from a device manufacturer or
distributor, they may have made changes or additions.

In particular, update or download servers may have been changed or additional functions
integrated. The respective manufacturer or provider is responsible for any data processing arising
from this. Please refer to their privacy notices.

## 8. Your rights

Where the legal requirements are met, you have the right to information about the data processed
about you (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing
(Art. 18) and, where applicable, data portability (Art. 20).

To exercise your rights, an informal message to the address given in section 2 is sufficient.

Because we do not store the IP address technically transmitted during the update check, we cannot
subsequently attribute a completed update request to a specific person or installation. Under
Art. 11 GDPR, we are not obliged to store additional personal data solely in order to identify
data subjects later on.

Right to object: Insofar as the processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object to the
processing at any time under Art. 21(1) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular situation.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The
competent authority is:
